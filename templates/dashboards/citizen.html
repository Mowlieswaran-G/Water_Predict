Our 3 Guarantees to the Public

🚫

Never Fake Data

We never invent measurements, estimate missing numbers, or substitute fake results when a station has not been sampled.

📅

Honest Historical Dates

If a water sample is older than 365 days, it is prominently labeled as "Historical" with the exact collection date shown.

🛡️

Unverified Ward Disclosure

Because government groundwater stations do not have official ward boundary mappings, we always disclose ward matches as unverified.
