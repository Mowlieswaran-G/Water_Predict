Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) Checklist

Mandatory verification protocols enforced prior to approving any municipal water report.

4 Protocols Active
1
Check Collection Date

If sample was collected over 365 days ago, verify that the report displays the mandatory "Historical" warning label.

2
Confirm Unit Integrity

Verify that concentrations are properly scaled (1 mg/L = 1,000 µg/L) and non-concentration units (like pH) are never converted.

3
Verify Ward Boundaries

Confirm that ward links are explicitly labeled as "Unverified" to protect citizens from false municipal tap water assumptions.

4
Ensure Human Sign-off

No report can be published without explicit sign-off by a designated municipal officer or authorized researcher.
